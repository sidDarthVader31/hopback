# HopBack Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 13 November 2026_

HopBack is a browser extension that lists the questions you asked in an AI chat and scrolls to the one you pick. This policy explains what it does with your information. The short version: it doesn't collect any.

## What HopBack collects

Nothing. HopBack has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. It makes no network requests of any kind.

## What HopBack reads

To build the list of your questions, HopBack reads the text of the messages you sent, as they are shown on the chat page you have open. That text:

- stays inside the page, in your browser's memory
- is never saved to disk
- is never sent anywhere
- is discarded when you leave or close the page

HopBack does not read the AI's answers, your account details, your chat history list, or anything on any other website.

## What HopBack stores

One number: the vertical position of the HopBack button, if you drag it. It is saved in your browser's local extension storage so the button stays where you put it. It never leaves your device and is deleted when you remove the extension.

## Where HopBack runs

Only on these sites: chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com, www.perplexity.ai, chat.deepseek.com, grok.com, copilot.com and copilot.microsoft.com. It does nothing on any other site.

## Sharing and selling

HopBack has no data to share or sell, and does neither.

## Permissions

- **storage**: to remember the button position described above.
- **Access to the sites listed above**: to find your questions on the page and show the HopBack button there.
